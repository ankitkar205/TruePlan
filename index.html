TRAI Consumer Protection (13th Amendment) Regulations, 2026

Notified on September 22, 2026 by the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI), telecom service providers are mandated to offer standalone Voice & SMS-only Special Tariff Vouchers (STVs) without compulsory data bundling for validities of 30 days or less. These vouchers must match equivalent durations of data-bundled plans and include same-date monthly renewals.

Also incorporates TRAI 66th Tariff Order 2022 (Mandatory 30-day and calendar month recharge). TRAI Notification open_in_new PIB Release open_in_new
